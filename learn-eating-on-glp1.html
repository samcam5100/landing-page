What to eat on a GLP-1

GLP-1 medications work best when paired with good nutrition. Because your appetite is lower, the food you do eat matters more. A few simple principles go a long way.

Make protein a priority

When you are eating less, getting enough protein helps protect muscle while you lose weight. Try to include a source of protein at each meal, such as eggs, fish, poultry, beans, or yogurt.

Stay hydrated

It is easy to drink less when you feel less hungry. Keep water nearby throughout the day, which also helps with common side effects.

Choose fiber and whole foods

Vegetables, fruit, and whole grains add fiber that supports digestion and helps you feel satisfied.

Eat slowly and stop when comfortable

Smaller, slower meals are easier to tolerate, and there is no need to finish a plate if you feel full. Your care team can help you build an approach that fits your life.
